01 — How it works

Five steps. You own the fourth.

Everything before approval is automated and checked. The decision about what goes out under your name is always yours.

  1. i.

    Find

    Apollo searches for the people you'd actually write to — recruiters, engineering managers, founders — at companies with recent postings for your target roles.

    Apollo search
  2. ii.

    Qualify

    Only people with a verified work email whose company has a live, matching posting are kept. Everyone else is skipped, with a reason.

    Rules, not vibes
  3. iii.

    Draft

    A short note built from your profile and that posting. Claude Code writes it with every tool switched off, then it's checked for the company, the role and your resume link.

    Claude Code
  4. iv.

    Approve

    One email per person: who they are, their LinkedIn, the posting, the exact message. Tap approve, tap reject, or just reply APPROVE.

    Your inbox
  5. v.

    Send

    That single letter goes out from your own Gmail, inside your daily limit, and lands in an audit log you can read any time.

    Gmail · SQLite
02 — The rules

Six rules it can't break.

  1. Nothing leaves without your yes.

    Every message needs an explicit approval, and approving one never approves another.

  2. It never makes things up.

    Drafts may only use your profile and a stored job posting. People are never invented.

  3. Only your reply counts.

    Approvals by email are read from the first line you typed, from your own address. Quoted text can't approve anything.

  4. Limits hold at the last moment.

    The daily cap, duplicate checks and your do-not-contact list are enforced right as a message is sent.

  5. The AI can write, not act.

    Drafting runs with every tool disabled, so outside data can shape words but never trigger anything.

  6. It stays on your machine.

    Local database, your own keys, and a dry-run mode until you decide to go live.

04 — Set it up

Clone it, connect your accounts.

There's no sign-up and no server of ours. ReachAI runs with your keys, for you only.

  1. Python 3.11 or newermacOS, Linux, or Windows with WSL.
  2. An Apollo.io API keySearching is free; each contact lookup costs about one credit, capped per run.
  3. A Google Cloud OAuth clientSo ReachAI can email you drafts and send from your Gmail.
  4. Claude Code, optionallyFor written drafts on your existing login. Without it, a fact-only template is used.
  5. Your real profileEmail, a public resume link, your experience and the roles you want.
~/ReachAI
# install
$ git clone https://github.com/AmanSingh-tech/ReachAI.git
$ cd ReachAI && python3 -m venv .venv
$ .venv/bin/pip install -e .
$ cp .env.example .env   # add APOLLO_API_KEY

# connect gmail (OAuth JSON in secrets/)
$ .venv/bin/reachai auth-gmail

# open your desk, then find people
$ .venv/bin/reachai serve
$ .venv/bin/reachai run

It starts in dry-run: approval emails are real, sends are simulated. Full guide in the README.

05 — Questions
Does it message people on LinkedIn?

No. LinkedIn has no public API for messages, and automating your account breaks its terms and can get it banned. ReachAI writes to people's verified work email and gives you their LinkedIn so you can look them up yourself.

Can it send anything without me?

No. Each message needs your approval — by button, in the dashboard, or by replying APPROVE from your own address. In dry-run mode even approved messages are only simulated.

Is this a hosted service?

No. You run it on your own computer with your own API keys and the data stays in a local database. This website only explains the project.

What does it cost to run?

ReachAI is free. Apollo charges credits for contact lookups (you set a cap per run), Gmail is free, and drafts use your existing Claude plan — or no AI at all if you choose the template.

Won't the notes sound generic?

Each one names the actual posting and company and uses your real experience. Because it can't invent details, it stays short and specific instead of flattering.
