How it works

From "who's hiring?" to a sent message, in five steps

You stay in the loop at the only step that matters: deciding what goes out under your name.

  1. Find

    Apollo finds people with the titles you choose at companies with recent postings for your target roles.

  2. Qualify

    Only people with a verified work email whose company has a matching live posting are kept.

  3. Draft

    Claude Code writes a short note using only your profile and that posting, then it's fact-checked.

  4. Approve

    You get one email per person with their LinkedIn, the job, and the exact message. Approve or reject.

  5. Send

    Only that approved message goes out from your Gmail, within your daily limit, and is logged.

Built to be trusted

Automation that never speaks for you without asking

Approval is mandatory

Nothing is sent without an explicit approval, and approving one message never approves another.

Grounded, not invented

Drafts may only use facts from your profile and a stored job posting. Targets are never made up.

Approve from your inbox

Click the button or just reply APPROVE. Only replies from your own address count, and quoted text never does.

Limits that hold

A daily send limit, duplicate checks, and a blocklist are enforced at the moment of sending.

Sandboxed AI

Claude Code drafts with every tool disabled, so outside data can shape text but never trigger actions.

Local-first

SQLite on your machine, keys in your own .env, and a dry-run mode until you're ready to go live.

Use it yourself

Set up ReachAI with your own accounts

ReachAI is a single-user tool you run on your own computer. There's no sign-up: clone it, connect your accounts, and you're in control.

  • Python 3.11+On macOS, Linux or Windows (WSL).
  • An Apollo.io API keyFor finding people. Searches are free; each contact lookup uses about one credit.
  • A Google Cloud OAuth clientSo ReachAI can email you approvals and send from your Gmail.
  • Claude Code (optional)For AI-written drafts on your existing login. Without it, a fact-only template is used.
  • Your real profileEmail, a public resume link, your experience and the roles you want.
Terminal
# 1. Install
git clone https://github.com/AmanSingh-tech/ReachAI.git
cd ReachAI
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -e .
cp .env.example .env    # add APOLLO_API_KEY

# 2. Connect Gmail (OAuth JSON in secrets/)
.venv/bin/reachai auth-gmail

# 3. Open the dashboard and finish the checklist
.venv/bin/reachai serve   # http://127.0.0.1:8000

# 4. Find people and get approval emails
.venv/bin/reachai run

Starts in dry-run: approval emails are real, sends are simulated. Switch to live sending once you're happy. Full setup guide in the README.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Does it message people on LinkedIn?

No. LinkedIn doesn't offer an API for sending messages, and automating your account breaks its terms and risks a ban. ReachAI emails people at their verified work address and shows you their LinkedIn profile so you can look them up yourself.

Can it send anything without me?

No. Every message needs your explicit approval, by button or by replying APPROVE from your own address. In dry-run mode, even approved messages are only simulated.

Is it a hosted service?

No. It runs on your own computer with your own API keys, and your data stays in a local database. This website only explains the project.

What does it cost to run?

ReachAI itself is free. Apollo charges credits for contact lookups (you set a cap per run), Gmail is free, and drafts use your existing Claude plan, or no AI at all if you prefer the template.

Will the messages sound generic?

Each draft names the actual job posting and company and uses your real experience. Because it can't invent details, it stays short and specific rather than flattering. You can edit your profile to change what it draws on.
